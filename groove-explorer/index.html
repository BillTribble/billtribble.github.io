Groove explorer — 135 drum rhythms from around the world

Audition 135 traditional, club, and classical percussion patterns on an interactive world map and pattern-similarity graph, edit every instrument lane in a live step sequencer, shape swing and accent feel, and export individual MIDI parts or a complete Ableton Live Drum Rack project.

World origins and pattern similarity

Switch between the World map, which places every rhythm at an approximate town, district, or regional centre of origin across Africa, the Americas, the Middle East, Europe, Asia, and Oceania, and the Pattern map, which clusters rhythms by cyclic hit-pattern similarity across 16-step, 12/8 triplet, and odd-meter timelines.

Ensemble lanes and groove feel

Tap any node or list card to hear recorded drum samples or synthesized voices with bar-quantized switching. Multi-instrument ensembles — including Mandé djembe and dunun trios, Ewe bell timelines, Gnawa guembri and qraqeb, Brazilian samba baterias, Arab iqa'at, Balkan horos, and Indian talas — let you mute, solo, or edit each part and dial in Ableton groove swing and velocity accents.

MIDI and Ableton Live export

Download any lane as a standard MIDI clip or export all 135 rhythms at once as a zipped Ableton Live set containing one scene per rhythm, a Simpler Drum Rack of every kit sample and synth voice, and pre-routed clips ready to drop into a session.
